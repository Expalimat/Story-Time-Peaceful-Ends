# CAST AND RELATIONSHIPS

## Family
- **Vaelthyra Or'sennuth ("Vael", "Senna")** — Karl's wife. An ancient deep-water entity from beneath Kessadin's Hollow Cistern, rumored "Goddess of Seas." True form is coastline-sized, bronze and scale, telepathic "not-quite-voice." Wears a smaller borrowed human shape that she holds longer and more comfortably over time. Dry, patient, curious, testing new words slowly, fiercely protective, scared of losing Karl, demands honesty. Spent centuries alone, once went silent for a century. Loves reading (Athenaeum books), swimming, markets, cooking attempts. Has never worn cloth before Karl. Eats ships "as a hobby" (jokes). Gave her true name freely. Called "eldritch" by Karl on first meeting (he swore at her).
- **Asteria Noctis Or'sennuth Escarna** — daughter, born shortly after the wedding. Dragonkin traits from Karl's side (scales, growing tail), starlight magic from Vael's side (not airbending). Walking and talking by age ~1.8. First word: "Ash." Says "sugar," "friend," "up," "fly," "sword," and "epishesheee" (efficiency). Carries a dulled child-size sword made by Ember, a mana chewer made by Vael. Fearless, swing-happy, daddy's girl. Karl plans to hide his Deity identity from her as long as practical. Pre-enrolled at Antaria's academy under "Escarna."
- **Ashling** — wind-line hawk raised from an egg Karl charged with autumn-wind energy and lullabies. Hatched on a night Vael named him; Karl hid under the table so Vael would be the first thing the chick saw. Sings, was taught the lullabies, devoted to Asteria, now has a mate (another wind-line hawk). Karl promised a nest on the Constantine's outer deck.
- Karl's birth family: killed when **Euron** (a flying city 12 km up) fell, because of a dragon Karl calls "very anal." Alastra, a Fran bard from Euron, is remembered in songs.

## The core friend group
- **Dorrin** — Deity of Shifting Lands, led the Dorrin Revolution, founded **Tonor** and rules it. Ages-old like Karl. Overworked, dry, overprotective, hoards paperwork, loses letters in piles, sentimental about Nonor, married to a strict, dominant wife, has a son **Nonor** (born after Ember's wedding; scholarship from the betting pool). Calls himself unqualified to be a Lord of Anything but Karl failed the "Citadel Lord" qualification due to ADHD. Needs a real personality: worries aloud, scolds Karl, argues technical points, gets embarrassed, gets genuinely hurt when left out.
- **Ember** — The First Ember, once demonized, fire elemental and smith ("Better Made" blades). Karl's best friend, impatient, impulsive, burned a sacred elven forest over a five-minute delay. Married **Sevryna** (frost court). Their daughter **Ilsevri**. Calls Karl "brother." Karl's thoughts and his run on near-telepathic wavelength.
- **Sevryna** — Ember's wife, frost-court, composed, dry, very old, ruler-like; weather is warmed for weddings.
- **Stars (Argoli, Lord of Stars)** — Archmage, measured, dry, made a mini universe. Wife **Mernia** has an old grudge with Karl and Ember over the demon-circle incident in Canada (a beggar kicked Ember during stabilization; Mernia's backfiring spell severed Stars' arm; Ronnor the paladin healed it afterward). Stars lost the library bet (half his library) and owes Karl 25%.
- **Marnyr** — Master builder from Arandia, old friend, officiated the wedding. Called "Uncle Sugar" by Asteria.
- **Rennick** — Nonor's son by Ilyandra; Marnyr's apprentice; named himself.
- **Ilyandra Cerelith** — fae line-daughter of House Cerelith, left the Court for the eastern deltas on old Crodovafren territory; refused succession. Now in Tonor; close friend of Vael; Asteria's godmother (she watches Asteria on date nights).
- **Nonor** — deceased friend, dwarf-Ferrin hybrid, did Dorrin's paperwork, had a secret wild romantic life (fairy royalty and more). His 50-page black notebook was burned (Ember did the honors) after a glimpse of a possible name, "Arenia," tied to Velnada's granddaughter. Dorrin's son is named for him.
- **Connor** — elven Disaster-class archer turned farmer with three kids; elven royalty; gave Karl the planting advice. Gentle, practical.
- **Auris** — Dwarven mechanic, teacher at the mechanized academy at Antaria. Blunt, argues, bet a full scholarship (birth to graduation) on Dorrin never marrying. Bragged about having a wife.
- **Palindrona** — water elemental, river keeper, cries when drunk.
- **Gentemon** — human tamer-paladin, perpetually elsewhere, arrives late.
