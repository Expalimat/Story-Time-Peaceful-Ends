## Ages

### 1. The Great Shift
A sudden, catastrophic movement of the world's tectonic plates causes continents and countries to collide and merge into a Pangaea-like supercontinent. Massive portions of civilization are destroyed, leaving ruins. The geological upheaval causes dramatic, unpredictable changes in global temperatures and climates.
Also called the First Catastrophe. It kills approximately 1.5 billion people, almost entirely the elderly. Entire countries are erased, cities destroyed by earthquakes and shifting land, coasts devastated. With much of the older population gone, an immense amount of accumulated knowledge is lost. Survivors are scattered across the new world among the ruins of the old one.

### 2. The Gates Disaster
Ten years before the Gates arrived, humanity began becoming attuned to the elements. The four elemental origins are Air, Fire, Water, and Earth, with many sub-elements. Only about one in three people can manifest an element, and each person specializes in exactly one.
Conflicts between elemental users become common. Then the Gates arrive. Through them come creatures of countless forms and horrors, Otherworlders from ruined worlds, and the sudden Immortalization of multiple people, eventually known as Deities. The world becomes a free-for-all. Humanity fights creatures, while Otherworlders fight humanity or each other. What begins as chaos becomes centuries of warfare. Generations are born into a world where war is normal.

### 3. The Great Demonic Surge
Centuries of bloodshed make monsters grow increasingly powerful. The strongest begin to become demonized. Ancient creatures grow stronger; others transform into far more dangerous beings. The demons grow powerful enough to overwhelm the factions fighting each other. The Aloi Alliance forms in response, a temporary alliance of long-time enemies. The alliance shatters the demonic front, but the victory is not theirs alone: the Elementals tear through the remaining demonic forces and end the Surge. The war, however, keeps ravaging the lands. The Surge becomes another layer on the centuries-long Gates Disaster.

### 4. The Dorrin Revolution
After the Surge, oppression of the surviving populations continues. Humans, Elmen, Stros, and Blerans rise against it, led by the Deity of Shifting Lands, **Dorrin**. The four races fight together under his leadership. The revolution's victory leads to the founding of **Tonor**, a massive city on the borders of the former Saudi region. Tonor becomes one of the larger cities of the new world. The revolution does not end the wider Gates Disaster.

### 5. The End of The Gates Disaster
After centuries, the Gates Disaster ends. The decision is born not of prosperity but of destruction: the beings fighting are gone, tired, or sick of senseless war. The old world is gone, the old civilizations mostly gone, and the survivors are remnants of populations that once numbered far more. The world that remains is built on the ruins of three catastrophes and centuries of war.

## Player Lore (original)
During the first years of the Elemental Surge, Karl, like many beings, became immortal with the passage of time. He could control one of the four Origin Elements: **Air**. Although an origin element, it was not special, only one of many. He fought through the eras and gained the epitaph **"The Deity of Wandering Skies"** for his immense control over air and wind (typhoons, tornadoes, weather phenomena) and his wandering lifestyle, never staying anywhere long.

Said to be one of the **24 Strongest Beings** in the entire world:

**4 Deities**
- The Deity of Living Earth
- The Deity of the Blazing Sky
- The Deity of Wandering Skies (Karl)
- The Deity of the First Anvil

**12 Other-Worlders**

**2 Humans**
- Argoli, The Lord of Stars (Sorcerer) — "Stars"
- Duran, Son of Vala (Barbarian)

**2 Ancients**
- The First Ember (Demonized Elemental) — Absolute Enemy of Wandering Skies (in the story, Ember is also Karl's close friend; see cast notes)
- The Last Cryptid (Komodo Dragon Cryptid)

**4 Monsters**
- Tropos the Sea Drake
- Argrovoncre, Lord of Blood (Demon Lord of France)
- Velnada, The Last Emperor Class Dragon
- Crodovafren, Horde of Plagues (an entire horde of monsters)

NOTE FROM THE STORY: In practice the user treats "the 22 Greats" as the invitees to the wedding, excluding Karl himself and the dragon (Velnada). Stars and Ember are among them. Crodovafren ("Crondo") and Tropos attended the wedding; so did the Last Cryptid.
