# PLACES, FACTIONS, AND WORLD FACTS

## Locations
- **Ashport Warrens** — patched ruin settlement, wardrums, tea-seller (name forgotten by Karl), where the story starts.
- **Halden's Siding** — rail-yard town with the inn **The Siding**; bartender told Karl about Tonor and Kessadin.
- **Kessadin** — Citadel at the tri-border near old Algeria; mixed human/Otherworlder; districts: Highspire (nobles), Aszurin Row (elemental academies), Hollow Cistern (old district around a deep well and the aquifer where Vael lives), Otherworlder district (Memory Guild, bent-light architecture), lower city, Athenaeum, Iron Ledger inn, market. Karl's first house was in Highspire.
- **Tonor** — Dorrin's coastal Citadel on the old Saudi border; industrializing, smog cleared by Karl; has a harbor guild, academies, hospital; new broadcast tower; Karl's house now sits on a rise overlooking the bay.
- **Frostlands** — Sevryna's territory in the far north. Wedding site of Ember and Sevryna; a first-frost ice ceremony ("the ice has to be honest").
- **Verdant Court** — fae Court in old-growth forest where Karl misread a letter and cratered the clearing.
- **Eastern river deltas** — Ilyandra's hiding place, former Crodovafren Horde territory.
- **Arandia** — old fallen Citadel of elementals and humans living in "kind of" harmony; three towns in a circle with a fountain courtyard at the center. Site of proposal and wedding. Never flew before; now being rebuilt to fly like Euron (12 m test lift completed, reinforced joints, shell layer planned, full altitude test pending).
- **Ashfall Reaches** — old stone Citadel with archives; holds Euron's remaining records and tech.
- **Antaria** — Antarctic Citadel Karl financed (90% of cost, 60% of library); houses Auris's academy, a large library, and the Lunar Knights.
- **Euron** — destroyed flying city 12 km up; Karl's birthplace; its engineering underlies the Constantine and house.
- **Eternally burning forest** — still smoldering; its Old One core was swapped for a demonized dragon core so the fires calm.
- **Rana Mountain Range** — site of a church-contained Old One core (from the one Karl killed).

## Magic and power systems
- **Elemental**: uses nature itself, convinces wind/water/fire/earth directly. Four Origins plus sub-elements. Only ~1 in 3 people are attuned.
- **Mana**: uses affinity to convince nature and natural order. Different from elemental.
- **Divinity**: separate again. Mana and divinity cores are incompatible; Karl's wind-channeling ships require matching energy types. Dorrin's ship, the **Nonor's Legacy**, uses a mana core plus a divinity backup core at 50% capacity for the other type.
- **Core purification**: no standard method. Karl "domineering flow," Stars brute force with mana, Ember burns, Auris almighty divine, Dorrin brick-by-brick structured. Holy water instantly purifies demon cores (but not harbinger cores, which brute force makes explode). Touching a corrupted core corrupts you. You only die or demonize if your reserves are empty and you absorb corrupted energy. Circulating fresh energy through a still-corrupted core can cause a "tsar bomba"-scale blast (four towns).
- **Memory resin**: Memory Guild extracts sensory imprints without removing memories. The Encradan memory (joy, not sorrow) and a pre-Gates childhood memory exist as crystals.
- **True names** carry leverage; sharing one is a major act of trust.
- **Helvan transmutation** converts an object into its elemental version, used when making beams.

## Technology
- Mana broadcasting (television) tower in Tonor, first signal tests had Dorrin awkwardly narrating "is this working."
- Hover-pad (Euron-derived platform) in two forms: a grocery cart and a mini battleship.
- **The Constantine the Great** — warship based on the "Neo Constantine the Great Class" concept art. Hundreds of weapons, Narvan rifles, Hell's Reach armor on doll guards. Canon size: about 75% of the Yamato's length (~197 m) at full width (~38.9 m). Interior: nursery, manga shelf, gaming lounge, workshop, navigation, meeting room, infirmary, tavern room, and an outer deck for Ashling's nest. Home-way protocol returns the ship home and goes battle-ready if Karl is asleep.
- **Frigate** — small repurposed hull used like a chauffeur car.
- **Flying House** — minor bastion platform carrying the Tonor house, garden, anrana tree, pond, play area, workshop, dock bays, with an Old One core in a shielded basement; currently grounded on its Tonor plot until Asteria is old enough for Antaria's elementary school.
- Weapons: **Narvan rifles** (anti-demon, from the subspecies of dargs called narvans), **Hell's Reach armor** (heat-resistant, death korps look).
- **Promise law**: Karl's promises are absolute; breaking one to him invites lethal or crippling retaliation.

## Antagonists/threat types seen
Gargans (earth spirits), Ridgeback Culls, Vharrek the Wallbreaker (killed at Kessadin's wall), the Old One (retold fight at the goddess church's rest stop cave), Crodovafren's horde, fraudulent engineer at Ashfall Reaches.
