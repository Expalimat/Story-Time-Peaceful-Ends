# PLAYER CHARACTER — KARL

## Names and titles
- Birth name: **Karl Uler Pablo Escarna** (officially re-registered in the story; "Uler" and "Pablo" are known only to the close group)
- Old war name: Karl Uler Pablo ex Papilio Lunae ("of the Moth of the Moon")
- Epitaph: **The Deity of Wandering Skies**. Old epitaph **"The Quiet Winds"** (earned after the Broken Promise of Arandia; kept secret from Vaelthyra for now)
- Vaelthyra calls him **Escarna**; he calls her **Vael** and **Senna** (pet name)
- True names are powerful: hearing his full birth name from anyone but Vael is uncomfortable, and full true names can be used for curses.
- Only ~100 people in the world personally know Karl.
- Titles: knighted; Grand Duke of the World Tree elven groves; Cardinal of the Goddess Church; former Count to the elves (pre-Surge, mostly forgotten); owner (honorary) of the Citadel **Antaria**; temporary teacher at Kessadin's elemental institution.

## Stats (from character JSON)
STR 74, DEX 94, CON 50, INT 80, WIS 50, CHA 31. HP 64. Speed 43. Stamina 20, Fatigue/Hunger/Thirst tracked 0/10.
Air-based damage base = (DEX + STR + INT) / 2 = 124.
Aging stopped at 21. Immune to poison "to the 5th degree" (self-inflicted training). Has ADHD (diagnosed by Stars in-world). Hyperfixates intensely (wrote a 530-page research manual and a 300-page house manual).

## Personality
Chaotic, blunt, funny, loyal, deflects with jokes, tells true things sideways while working. Treats promises as absolute. Weak to sincere compliments. Hates apologizing (can count his apologies on one hand). Underplays danger because he has survived so much. Loves manga, games, flying ships, cooking (learning), gardening, flute. Wants a quiet life with his family. Does not care about his titles.
Habit: says "Efficient" about nearly everything (his daughter now says "epishesheee").
Habit: buries the lede when telling dangerous stories; Vael asked him to lead with the ending ("it turned out fine").

## Combat identity
Airbender, flight, vortex, wind lance, compressed wind blasts, breaks the sound barrier. Can wrap himself in warm wind, air bubbles underwater, and thin spring wind. Purifies cores through "domineering flow." Fights with 2x hardwood kali sticks and later a rapier. Light set (formal black military jacket and cape) and Medium set (plate carrier, tactical gear).

## Skill cards (from the JSON)
- **Gale Severance** (cost 2, Unique/Retain): generates 4 Air Current each turn, 1 Wind Barrier that turn, 3 Momentum per attack card in hand.
- **Great Weather Storm** (cost 3): Humidity 4, Rain 4, Damp 3.
- **Wind Burst** (cost 0, basic): 120% air damage, 1 Weakness.
- **Wind Barrage** (cost 1): 175% air damage to all enemies, Pierce, draws Focus Shot if only one enemy, -2 Air Current.
- **Focus Shot** (drawn only): 400 true damage, 2 Severe Wound, 2 Agony.
- **Critical Burst** (cost 1): 250% air damage, +25% per bad effect on the enemy.
- **Sound Boom** (cost 1): Deafness, Stun, Confusion to all enemies, -2 Air Current. Capped at 1 copy because 2 copies made an AoE stun-lock.
- **Spatial Collapse** (cost 0, Retain/Unique): only usable vs exactly 1 enemy; 25 self-damage, Vacuum for 3 sets, injures a body part; 1350% air damage.
- **Deity of the Wandering Skies** (upgrade): gain Advance every 2 turns; all air attacks scale on (Dex+Str+Int)/2.
- **All Are Beneath Me** (Ego, 7 EP): needs 5+ Advance; consumes all; 300% air damage per Advance; Exhausted for 15 turns.
Full original card text is in assets/source_docs/Karl_character_sheet.json.

## Self-imposed promises to Vael
- Never drop below **25%** of his elemental energy reserve.
- Tell her **before** doing something dangerous, not after.
- Lead dangerous stories with the ending.
- Count harming anyone, even a little, as a reason to tell her first.
- Weekly or on-purpose story sharing of his past, one story at a time.
